Technical Architecture & Analysis

A deep breakdown of the Kotlin & Jetpack Compose modules powering FocusFlow DXT.

⚙️ Core Service-Driven State Machine

Unlike standard countdown applications that terminate when swiped away, FocusFlow DXT is engineered around a robust Foreground Service (`TimerService.kt`). It serves as the single source of truth for timer states, lap details, and active trackers:

  • Compose State Binding: Binds foreground states with Jetpack Compose views using Kotlin MutableStateFlow, enabling real-time UI synchronization.
  • Dual Interception Engine: Combines Android UsageStatsManager background thread loops (polled at set grace thresholds) with a fallback AccessibilityService listener to maximize blocking reliability.
  • Simulated Hardware Display Modes: The rendering architecture supports four distinct faceplate configurations: Classic Digital LCD, Analog ticking hands, Minimalist Smart Watch dashboard, and vintage Pocket Watch casing.

🌱 Simulated Forest & Audio Synthesizers

FocusFlow DXT merges classic timer aesthetics with gamified progression systems and custom ambient audio drivers:

  • Sunshine Forest Plantations: Focus minutes generate Scholar Coins. Use them in the virtual Sunshine Shop to unlock custom trees (Pine, Sakura, Bonsai) or purchase special fertilizers to accelerate growth logs.
  • Ambient Soundscapes: Integrated media player simulates soothing environment tracks including White Noise, Ocean Waves, and Forest rustles to isolate student workspace acoustics.
  • Sensor Calibration: Interfaces with device ambient temperature hardware sensors (`Sensor.TYPE_AMBIENT_TEMPERATURE`) to render real room heat, with built-in digital offset sliders for calibration.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Trust Shield

100% Private, Local-First, and Serverless: FocusFlow DXT does not utilize telemetry tracking or cloud database networks.

  • No External Tracking: Distraction logs, application usage times, and blacklisted app names are stored purely locally inside encrypted SharedPreferences.
  • Zero Telemetry: No analytics trackers (like Firebase Analytics or Flurry) are embedded, making the app immune to data leaks.
  • Offline Independence: The application does not require internet permissions to lock apps or calculate scholar XP levels.

⚠️ Hardware Limits & OS Optimization

Operating system sandboxing and background limitations may affect functionality on specific Android skins:

  • OEM Sleep Policies: Strict skins (MIUI/Xiaomi, ColorOS/Oppo, OxygenOS) may terminate background services. Users must turn off battery optimizations manually.
  • Overlay Limitation: Extreme mode warning overlays require the "Display over other apps" system overlay permission, which must be enabled during initial startup settings.
  • System-Blocked Access: Certain system-secured settings, dialers, and package screens cannot be intercepted due to core Android security policies.

💎 100% Free & Open-Source Policy

FocusFlow DXT is free software built for students, scholars, and researchers.

  • No Advertisements: Zero display banners, interstitial advertisements, or third-party promotional content.
  • No In-App Purchases: Virtual coins used for forest plantations are earned exclusively through focus time. No pay-to-win hooks.
  • MIT Licensed Code: The codebase is fully transparent, allowing audit verification, forks, or packaging adjustments without restrictions.

Offline Smart App Interceptor (Focus Guards)

Keep social feeds and short-video distractions away. FocusFlow works completely offline, calling local package queries without uploading usage histories to third-party servers.

  • Extreme Alarm Profile: Plays custom warning buzzing sounds and displays a fullscreen lockdown window until the blacklisted application is closed.
